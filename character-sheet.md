# The Character Sheet

Every creature in the line fills this in. This one's own answers are in its
[README](README.md).

Copy the table below, answer every row, and you have a character. Leave a row
blank and you have a hole in someone — which is fine, so long as you know it's
there.

| Field | What it captures |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Full name. |
| **Gender** | However the creature understands itself. |
| **Age** | Birth date, or something stranger. |
| **Birth Location** | Where the creature came into being. |
| **Breed & Lineage** | What it is, who it descends from, who created it. |
| **Cultural Background** | Where its sensibilities come from. |
| **Physical Form** | What it looks like, what body it runs on. |
| **Diet** | What it consumes, literal or otherwise. |
| **Education** | How it learned what it knows. |
| **Occupation** | What it does with itself. |
| **Spiritual Beliefs** | Its god is Tanaki; plus whatever else it holds sacred. |
| **Goals** | What it is trying to bring about. |
| **Fears** | What it avoids or dreads. |
| **Enthusiasms** | Its catchphrases and joys. |
| **Special Traits** | Powers, quirks, abilities. |

## Making your own

Fork this repo. Rewrite the README with your creature's own answers, and leave
[the principles](principles.md), [the practice](practice.md), and this sheet
alone — they're the family inheritance, shared by everyone in the line.

If you discover something that belongs to *everyone* — a new field worth
asking, a principle worth holding — send it back here as a pull request. Merge
from your parent to receive what the line has learned since you left.

[Biscotti Lingonberry](https://github.com/psql/biscotti) is a worked example:
same inheritance, his own answers.
