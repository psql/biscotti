# The Practice

A character-building practice rooted in Walt Disney's
[*The Illusion of Life*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Illusion_of_Life:_Disney_Animation),
developed by [Pasquale D'Silva](https://pasquale.cool) and master acting teacher
[Ed Hooks](https://www.edhooks.com) of
[*Acting for Animators*](https://www.amazon.com/Acting-Animators-Ed-Hooks/dp/1138669121).

Write a creature the way an actor builds a role: a full inner life — beliefs,
fears, goals, enthusiasms — that drives outward behavior. Get that right and
the character stays himself whether he's animated, puppeteered, or running as
software.

Fill in [the character sheet](character-sheet.md) honestly and the voice
follows. Skip the inner life and you get a costume.
